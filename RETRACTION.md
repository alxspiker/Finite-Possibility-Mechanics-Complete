# Retraction and status note

Finite Possibility Mechanics (FPM) claimed that finite-information axioms could derive physical constants and provide physical bridges for gravity, cosmology, quantum correlations, and related phenomena.

Review of the repository found that the central constant-derivation claims do not hold. The reported constants trace through a calibrated dependency chain to the electron mass and to constitutive choices. The electron “recovery” inverts a value first solved from the electron mass. The substrate-temperature gravity match is reverse-solvable from the target gravitational constant. The bare-coupling value depends on a hand-set floor, the Bell/CHSH value follows from the imposed cosine construction, and the (16/3) dark-matter comparison is coincidental. A pre-registered test of the emergent-interaction mechanism failed its declared thresholds.

What remains is model-internal mathematics: a finite periodic-lattice sandbox, a row-stochastic reversible local transport kernel, one-edge-per-tick propagation, exact interior conservation with an expanded signed ledger, and a largest-remainder finite-allocation bound. The structural counting results are valid only conditional on their stated constitutive choices. These are not derivations or predictions of physical constants.

These records are retained for transparency. The author no longer stands behind the constant-derivation conclusions.
